# The Vault: itch.io launch kit

Everything you need to publish The Vault on itch.io, in the order the "Create new project" page asks for it.

---

## 1. Basics

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The Vault |
| **Project URL** | `the-vault` (your page becomes kamo89.itch.io/the-vault, or whatever your username is) |
| **Short description / tagline** | Real cases. Real evidence. Can you make the call? |
| **Classification** | Games |
| **Kind of project** | HTML (you'll have a file that will be played in the browser) |
| **Release status** | Released |

## 2. Pricing

**Choose: "No payments"** for now, or **"Donate"** if you want a tip button from day one. Leave the minimum at R0 / $0.

Keep it free. Real cases should stay free to play. If you add paid fictional case packs later, that's when to revisit pricing.

## 3. Uploads

1. Click **Upload files** and choose `the-vault-itch.zip`.
2. Tick **"This file will be played in the browser"**.
3. Under **Embed options**:
   - **Viewport dimensions:** 1280 × 800
   - Tick **Mobile friendly** (orientation: Default)
   - Tick **Fullscreen button**
   - Tick **Enable scrollbars**

## 4. Details

### Description (paste this into the description box)

> **Real cases. Real evidence. Can you make the call?**
>
> The Vault is a detective game from The Crime Vault. You open a case file, read the evidence, and test every claim against it. Pin your clues to a corkboard, tie them together with red string, and when you're ready, make your call. You only get one shot.
>
> **Five cases on file**
>
> - **000 · Lights Out at Kiln.** A fictional training case. A gallery owner is killed in Maboneng, Johannesburg, minutes after load-shedding knocks out the CCTV. Four suspects, all with a reason.
> - **001 · The De Zalze Killings.** Stellenbosch, 2015. A family is attacked inside a guarded golf estate. The surviving son says a masked intruder did it.
> - **002 · The ABC Murders.** Gauteng, 1994–95. Women are found murdered across Atteridgeville, Boksburg and Cleveland. One killer or many?
> - **003 · The Frederick Disappearance.** Colorado, 2018. A pregnant mother and her two daughters vanish overnight.
> - **004 · The Golden State Killer.** California, 1975–86. Decades of crimes, then 30 years of silence. Follow the trail to a name.
>
> **How it plays**
>
> - **Evidence locker:** read each item. Some evidence only turns up once you've read something else.
> - **Statements:** test claims against the evidence. Expose a lie and the story changes. Some claims can't be disproved, and wrong guesses cost you.
> - **Investigation board:** drag your clues around and connect them with red string.
> - **Timeline:** builds itself from the times in the evidence you've found.
> - **Verdict:** lock in your answer and your three strongest pieces of evidence. Then find out what really happened.
>
> **About the real cases**
>
> Four of the five cases are real, solved cases with a conviction or guilty plea. They involve the murder of real people, including children. Every fact is taken from court records and news reports, and sources are listed at the end of each case. There's no graphic detail. In real cases, the wrong answers are theories that were considered at the time, never real innocent people. Each case ends by naming the people who were killed.
>
> **Play anywhere**
>
> The Vault runs in your browser on desktop and mobile. You can also install it as an app from the website (Install the app button, or Add to Home Screen on iPhone) and play offline.

## 5. Community and visibility

- **Community:** Comments. This lets players leave feedback on the page.
- **Visibility:** start with **Draft**, open the page, play it once in the embed to check it works, then switch to **Public**.

## 6. Content and audience

itch.io has a content/age section. Mark the content as including **violence (non-graphic) and real-world crime themes**. This is the honest description and protects the page from being reported.
